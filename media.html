How UV light can help to keep a brain slice alive

A science explainer on the original Braincubator study, describing how circulating the incubation medium through a separate 254 nm UV chamber, together with cooling to 15–16 °C, limited bacterial growth while keeping the brain tissue shielded from UV exposure.

Eric Kontos reports on the Braincubator developed at Western Sydney University, describing how the incubation system extended live-tissue viability from about six hours to at least 24 hours by controlling temperature and pH while using UVC filtration to keep bacterial levels low.
